# Brag Plan: CDX

## What is this app?
CDX is an autonomous terminal-native AI coding assistant for Linux that couples ultra-fast 500+ tok/s Groq LPU inference with local OS execution, a 4-node concurrent Sub-Agents Swarm DAG, and 12 specialized domain agents inside a sleek OLED dark workspace — created by Saad Kashif.

## The angle
The premise: your entire engineering team fits inside one dark terminal window. While other tools chat, CDX *executes* — orchestrating 4 autonomous agents in parallel, streaming production code at 528 tok/s, and taking over your mouse and keyboard when needed. The hook is the contrast between the calm, minimal OLED aesthetic and the explosive multi-agent swarm happening underneath.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Pure black screen. A single emerald cursor blinks. Then a mission directive types itself out character by character: *"Build an AI-powered SaaS MVP"*. The typing sound is crisp mechanical keyboard Foley. The ambient particle starfield fades in behind the text.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The "Launch Sub-Agents Swarm" button fires — all 4 DAG nodes (Architect, Backend, Frontend, Auditor) pulse cyan simultaneously, then lock to emerald green checkmarks one by one.
- Token stream at 528 tok/s — syntax-highlighted code cascading down the chat pane with a visible speed counter.
- The 12 Specialist Agent cards fan out in the Agents Hub — each with its own color-coded gradient (violet, teal, cyan, orange).

## Outro / punchline
Snap cut to minimal brand card on pure OLED black:
**CDX** · *Autonomous AI Desktop for Linux*
`sudo apt install cdx` · Created by Saad Kashif.
The emerald glow pulses once, then holds.

## User flow worth showing
- **Beat 1 (Entry)**: The Sub-Agents Swarm tab — user types or selects a mission preset.
- **Beat 2 (Key Action)**: Swarm launches — 4 concurrent nodes process with real-time status telemetry, procedural audio chime fires.
- **Beat 3 (Result)**: Synthesized deliverable materializes; user clicks "Send to Live Chat" to interact with the output immediately.

## Audio direction
- Role: cinematic support with electronic textures
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3 (110 BPM, cinematic energy)
- Music treatment: Fade in from 0.0s at 60% volume, build to 80% at swarm launch (4.0s), sustain through highlights, duck to 40% under brand card (16s), fade out by 20s
- Music cue guidance: Bundled preset at `cues/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.json`. Key strong cues: 8.74s (1.00) for swarm activation reveal, 13.11s (0.98) for agent hub fan-out, 17.47s (0.99) for brand card snap. Beat grid: 0.56s intervals for sequential card arrivals.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; emerald glow intensity on cards and borders breathes with music RMS energy. Particle canvas opacity subtly responds to bass frequency. No waveform bars or equalizer visuals.
- SFX posture: sparse and professional; motion-matched clicks and whooshes only at key moments
- Audio-coupled moments:
  - Scene 1: typing sound — crisp key ticks synced to character-by-character text reveal
  - Scene 2: swarm launch — procedural chime or impact hit on button press; status node transitions get soft UI clicks
  - Scene 3: code stream — subtle digital cascade texture
  - Scene 4: brand card — single deep logo hit with reverb tail
- Restraint rule: Audio must never feel busy or cluttered. Each sound earns its place. Silence between hits is as important as the hits.
